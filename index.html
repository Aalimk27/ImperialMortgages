    ResidentResident mortgages+

    For salaried and self-employed buyers living in the UAE. Up to 80% financing on a first home, fixed or variable, with salary transfer and non-salary-transfer options compared side by side.

    • Fixed periods from 1 to 5 years
    • Self-employed: 2 years of audited financials or bank statements
    • Employed: Minimum salary from AED 15,000 at most banks

    Buying in Dubai from London, Mumbai or Riyadh. We work with the banks that lend to overseas buyers and handle document attestation and remote signing.

    • Typically up to 50–60% financing
    • Freehold areas only

    Your off-plan unit is nearly complete and the final payment is due. We line up a mortgage in time for handover so the developer is paid and the title transfers to you.

    • Rate-review reminders before your fix ends

    Ijara and Murabaha structures from Islamic banks and Islamic windows of conventional lenders, compared on the same basis as conventional mortgages.

    • Profit rate instead of interest
    • Fixed and variable profit rates

    Offices, retail units and warehouses bought personally or through a company, including free-zone entities.

    • Usually 50–60% financing
    • Company financials and trade licence required

    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    How much deposit do I need as an expat?

    At least 20% of the price for a first home up to AED 5 million, and 30% above that. UAE nationals need 15% and 25%. On top of the deposit, budget roughly 7% of the price for fees, including the 4% DLD transfer fee.

    Can I get a mortgage if I don't live in the UAE?

    Yes. Several banks lend to non-residents in freehold areas, usually up to 50–60% of the value. Expect to provide bank statements, proof of income and a credit report from your home country.

    What is the debt burden ratio?

    The Central Bank caps all your monthly debt repayments, including the new mortgage, at 50% of your monthly income. Our borrowing calculator uses this rule.

    Fixed or variable?

    Fixed rates hold for 1 to 5 years, then move to a variable rate linked to EIBOR plus a margin. Variable rates track EIBOR from day one. We model both over your expected holding period.

    What does your service cost?

    The initial consultation and comparison are free. Our fee is agreed in writing before we submit any application, so there are no surprises at transfer.
